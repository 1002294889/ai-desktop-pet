# Rigged 3D Test Pet Asset Notice

`assets/rigged-demo.glb` is an original development asset generated specifically
for this repository by `tools/generate-skeletal-demo.mjs`.

It contains no downloaded character model, texture, motion-capture data, or
third-party character design. It is intentionally simple and exists only to
verify the application's GLB, SkinnedMesh, skeleton, and AnimationClip pipeline.

The generated clips are: Idle, Walk, Sit, Sleep, Wake, Happy, Angry, Jump, Wave,
and Talk.

`assets/animations/celebrate.glb` is also an original development asset. It is
generated by `tools/generate-external-animation.mjs` and contains only a small
compatible motion skeleton plus the `Celebrate` keyframe clip. Its deliberately
different bone names exercise the manifest-driven retargeting path. The clip has
no downloaded model, motion-capture data, texture, or third-party animation.

Both generated development assets and their source generators are dedicated to
the public domain under CC0 1.0. See https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
